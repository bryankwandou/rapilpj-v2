# RapiLPJ: Pre-Submission Audit for Student Organization Activity Reports
**Team:** Tide Ledger  
**Case:** Case 1: Smarter Campus and Learning  
**Members:** Vincentius Bryan Kwandou (leader), Valentino Filemon Limer, Putra Purwanugraha Tengbunan, Marvel Harjosetio  

## 1. Problem
Student treasurers manage campus funds without accounting training, under bylaws that require a physical stamp, an exact ledger balance, a meal-attendance ratio, a revenue stamp when a receipt's nominal value exceeds Rp5,000,000 (Law 10/2020), tax withholding, and a deadline. At Brawijaya Computer Science, an incomplete report freezes the next disbursement and the peer learning that depends on it [6]. Study hours go to mechanical corrections. The rule arrives as a rejection, so it is never practiced as fiscal judgment.

## 2. Intended Users
Primary users are treasurers and secretaries of BEM and clubs. The secretary keeps the attendance list the meal rule reads; the treasurer confirms every figure. Secondary users are faculty verifiers (Biro Kemahasiswaan and student-council auditors) on a pre-disbursement desk review. RapiLPJ records that review. It does not approve or release funds.

## 3. Supporting Observations or Evidence
Siada and Tungadi (2021) report that BKAM at Universitas Atma Jaya Makassar tracks student-activity portfolios through manual, unstandardized records, and they design an information system for that portfolio [1]. The citation stops there. Faculty rules then diverge: FIB UB requires stamped receipts and a balanced ledger [2]; FEB UGM requires a Rp10,000 stamp on any receipt exceeding Rp5,000,000 under Law 10/2020, plus a photocopy of thermal slips [3]; ITS Interior Design enforces a 10-day deadline [4]; ITS Biology requires separate lines and tax inclusion [5]. A 2026 FILKOM UB circular records officers failing tax withholding and reconciliation [6].

## 4. Proposed Solution and Core User Flow
RapiLPJ turns the rejection into a gated practice run. (1) Perceive, then confirm. Gemini 2.0 Flash reads one receipt photo into JSON (date, vendor, nominal, line type) and does no arithmetic. Each field sits beside the photo; the treasurer confirms or corrects it. Unconfirmed JSON cannot enter the ledger, so a misread Rp50,000 is never audited as Rp500,000. (2) Deterministic kernel on confirmed fields only, stored as a portable .lpj: duplicate invoice key; date outside the event window; missing stamp when nominal exceeds Rp5,000,000; variance above 10% of the approved line. Meal portions and PPh 21 are not inferred. Before the pilot, two faculty advisors sign one UAJM rule card. A meal line fails when claimed portions exceed the attendance count multiplied by the card's portions-per-person; an honorarium line fails when the withholding rate is not the card's PPh 21 rate. No rate on the card returns "preset gap"; the engine does not invent one. (3) Gated literacy. A failed check locks export until every quiz item matches a locked key. The stamp key is the statute: Rp5,000,000 exactly is not dutiable; any nominal above it is (Law 10/2020, Art. 3). A template fills the variance addendum from the failed fields. Gemini does not draft it. (4) Desk-check file, not disbursement. Export is an indexed receipt PDF plus a SHA-256 over the confirmed ledger, quiz result, and addendum. The verifier opens it locally; the screen recomputes the hash, writes "lolos desk-check" or itemized notes back into the .lpj, and hashes again. That returned file is the record. Hash match is immediate. Notes take as long as the reading. No control releases money.

## 5. How It Differs from Existing Alternatives
A pure LLM mis-adds a balance sheet. A spreadsheet will not bind a receipt image to a hash, block export on a failed quiz, or refuse to guess a missing tax rate. A campus portal only collects a finished file. RapiLPJ keeps one boundary: Gemini perceives, a person confirms every figure, and arithmetic, statutory checks, quiz keys, and addendum text are deterministic client-side code. A bad read cannot become a passed audit.

## 6. Planned Technologies
Perception: Gemini 2.0 Flash Multimodal, client-side BYOK, JSON only, one session. Before the pilot, ten held-out receipt photos must round-trip through extraction, human confirm, and the kernel. Kernel: open-source JavaScript, locked templates, FileReader, .lpj JSON, SHA-256. Presentation: accessible HTML5/CSS3, bilingual EN/ID, light/dark, static on Vercel, no application database. The upload screen states the limit: RapiLPJ stores no dossier, but the image is sent to Google's Gemini API under the treasurer's own key for that session and is not archived by the app.

## 7. Expected Benefit and Development Plan
No reduction percentage and no promised review SLA. With 30 treasurers and the same two advisors at UAJM, one real LPJ logs five numbers: treasurer minutes, mechanical defects before versus after the kernel, fields corrected after Gemini, quiz score before versus after the gate, and verifier minutes from open to returned file. Semifinal scope is one receipt, the confirm step, the signed card, the quiz gate, and the local desk-check. Final Day adds multi-page batching and a QR that re-checks the advisor's mark in the returned file. A rule change replaces the card, not the kernel.
